    <div class="features-page-hero">
      <p class="features-page-eyebrow">Capabilities</p>
      <h2>Everything you need at the edge, without the usual overhead</h2>
      <p class="section-lead">Leafcuttr groups the features that matter most during evaluation: how it connects, how it survives, and how it stays observable when conditions get messy.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="features-story">
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">Connect</span>
        <h3>Multi-Protocol Gateway</h3>
        <p>Native support for MQTT, HTTP, and Kafka means teams can integrate without adding proxies or extra layers.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">Connect</span>
        <h3>Built-in Schema Registry</h3>
        <p>No need for external services. Schema metadata stays with the broker, keeping producers and consumers aligned as message formats evolve.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">Run</span>
        <h3>Lightweight &amp; Efficient</h3>
        <p>Leafcuttr runs on constrained devices with low CPU and memory usage.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">Run</span>
        <h3>Resilient by Design</h3>
        <p>Flaky networks and intermittent connectivity do not stop local streaming from continuing.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">See</span>
        <h3>Observability Included</h3>
        <p>Metrics and monitoring are included out of the box, so operators can see what the broker is doing in the field.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="card feature-story-card">
        <span class="badge">Move</span>
        <h3>Async Replication</h3>
        <p>When connectivity is available, data can sync to the cloud mothership without blocking the local workload.</p>
      </article>
    </div>

    <div class="features-highlight card">
      <div class="features-highlight__content">
        <span class="mode-tag">Coming in v1.0</span>
        <h3>Filter, Forward &amp; Aggregate Streams</h3>
        <p>This roadmap item will add lightweight transformation closer to the device so teams can cut bandwidth and keep local decisions fast.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="features-highlight__note">
        <strong>Why it matters</strong>
        <p>It moves more value closer to the device, where edge teams can reduce noise before data leaves the site.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
